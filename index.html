    Equity Cash Flow Equations

    These dynamic equations display the present value of a stock and expected future dividend payments based on your inputs. The equation shown updates to match the selected model.

    Constant Dividend
    PV t = Div t+1 r
    PVt = Divt+1 ÷ r
    Constant Dividend Growth
    PV t = Divt (1+g) r − g = Divt+1 r − g
    PVt = Divt(1+g) ÷ (r − g) = Divt+1 ÷ (r − g)
    Changing Dividend Growth
    PV 0 = ∑ i=1 n Divt (1+gs) i (1+r) i + ∑ j=n+1 ∞ Divt+n (1+gl) j (1+r) j
    PV0 = Σ[i=1 to n] Divt(1+gs)i÷(1+r)i + Σ[j=n+1 to ∞] Divt+n(1+gl)j÷(1+r)j
